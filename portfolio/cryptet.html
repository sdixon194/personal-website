Cryptet

Cryptet is a string quintet that arranges and plays video game music. Since their founding at the 2025 Super MAGFest convention, I've been providing video and audio recording as well as general band management services for them. I've facilitated remote and live recordings for their videos, as well as handled some video editing and sound mixing.

All of their arrangements are created by Cryptet and you can see all their videos on YouTube.

No generative AI/LLMs were used or consulted for this project.
