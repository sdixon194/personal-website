Biography of the Chosen Undead

Biography of the Chosen Undead: The Dark Souls Story was a submission for Bandai-Namco's video competition in preparation of the release of Dark Souls 3. The rules were to create a five minute video that recapped the events of the first two Dark Souls games. The video won the Grand Prize of the competition and was featured on Kotaku and received endorsement from VaatiVidya.

The video was written, directed, and edited by me. A friend provided the voice over and role of Jace Goodley, and I played Bartholomew Underhill.

No generative AI/LLMs were used or consulted for this project.
