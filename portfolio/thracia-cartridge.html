Thracia 776 Translated Cart

Fire Emblem: Thracia 776 is a Fire Emblem game for Nintendo's Super Nintendo that was only released in Japan, never receiving an English translation. However, there are fan translations that you can patch a ROM with. For Christmas 2025, I thought it would be a fun idea to put a translated copy of the game on a physical SNES cart for my wife, who is a huge Fire Emblem fan, so she could play the game on original hardware.

This proved to be a much larger project than anticipated, as I discovered there's a lot more to having a patched game run on original hardware than just flashing an old SNES cart. Using a guide I found from Mouse Bite Labs, I obtained all the parts for building a cart from scratch (PCB board, EPROMs, SRAM, various resistors, transistors and ancillary chips) and the hardware needed to write to program the EPROMs, as well as a cart shell and label. I stealthily spent a month soldering together a test board to ensure the EPROM programming worked, before building the final cart and affixing everything.

My wife spent many hours playing what I understand to be one of the hardest Fire Emblem games you can play on our SNES. Merry Christmas!

And it worked!

No generative AI/LLMs were used or consulted for this project.
